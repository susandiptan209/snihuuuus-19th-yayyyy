# SNIHUUUUU's Soundscape

Project: Birthday Website for SNIHUUUUU

Overall requirement: Fully mobile-optimized (she'll open this on her phone, not a laptop) — single-page scrolling or fade-transition site, HTML/CSS/JS, sound-based experience.

Page 1 — Landing / Cover

Full-screen photo (I'll provide)

A gentle prompt overlay: "🎧 Turn your volume up" — with a subtle animated icon (pulsing headphones or speaker) so she notices it before pressing play

Background song starts playing here (I'll provide the track)

Instead of a generic "See your wish" button, use something more personal/quirky — e.g. "Tap if you dare" / "Unlock your surprise" / "One tap, one wish" (I want something that matches her personality — not a cliché line)

Page 2 — The Main Wish

Triggered after the button tap

Background: a second photo, made translucent/blurred (soft glassy effect)

Foreground: the main birthday wish text, animated in (fade/typewriter style)

Song changes here to a second track (I'll provide) — smooth crossfade from song 1, not an abrupt cut

(Optional) A photo collage before or after this page — a grid/scattered polaroid-style layout of multiple photos

Page 3 — Closing / Solo Photo

Full-screen solo photo of her

Closing message overlaid, styled elegantly, something like:
"Cheers to your 19th year — your last teen year 🥂 I hope and pray the good Lord keeps you safe, happy, and healthy. Lots of love, your Bachu 💛"

Keep it as the final, lingering screen — no further navigation needed after this

Technical notes for the AI building it:

Mobile-first responsive design (portrait orientation priority)

Smooth transitions between pages (fade/slide), not abrupt jumps

Autoplay audio with a tap-to-start fallback (browsers block autoplay with sound until user interacts — so the volume-up prompt page can double as the interaction that starts playback)

Placeholder slots clearly marked for: photo 1, photo 2 (blurred bg), collage photos, solo photo, song 1, song 2

Keep the tone: playful, personal, a little dramatic/cinematic — not corporate or templated
